FUNERAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

OVERVIEW
The Memorial Care Management System is a web-based application designed to streamline the management of funeral services, 
inventory, customer transactions, and administrative operations. The system helps funeral homes efficiently manage products, 
services, customer records, orders, payments, and inventory through a centralized platform.

FEATURES
Customer Features
- User registration and login
- View product details and pricing
- Place orders and reservations
- Manage customer profiles
- Track order status
- View Transaction History
Administrative Features
- Dashboard with business analytics
- Inventory management
- Product and service management
- Customer management
- Order management
- Payment tracking
- Revenue monitoring
- User account management
Inventory Management
- Local casket Inventory
- Imported casket Inventory
- Vehicle Inventory
- Material Inventory
- Stock Monitoring
- Inventory Reports
Additional Features
- Google Maps Integration for location tracking
- Interactive map display for funeral home branches and service locations
- Real-time chat and messaging between customers and administrators
