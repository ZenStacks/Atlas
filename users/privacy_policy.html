Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Alfonso Somo Funeral Homes respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact number
  • Home address
  • Account login information
  • Funeral service preferences and requests
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Messages submitted through our contact forms

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Process funeral service requests and bookings.
  • Manage customer accounts.
  • Provide customer support and communication.
  • Process payments and generate receipts.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared when necessary to:

  • Process authorized transactions.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of our customers and business.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials. We recommend choosing strong passwords and not sharing login information with others.

7. Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. Requests may be submitted through our contact channels.

9. Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, maintain login sessions, and analyze website performance.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging funeral services. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental or guardian consent.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
