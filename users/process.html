OUR PROCESS

From Arrangement to Completion

We provide a straightforward process designed to help families focus on what matters most.

01
STEP 01

Get in Touch

Contact Alfonso Somo Funeral Services through our website or directly with our staff. Share your needs with us and we will assist you with the next steps.

02
STEP 02

Provide Information

Provide the necessary information about the deceased and the service requirements. Our team will help make sure the details are complete and accurate.

03
STEP 03

Choose Services

Choose the funeral services and packages that best suit your family's needs. You can also select your preferred casket and choose between a Standard or Premium flower arrangement. If you have a specific flower or customized setup in mind, you can contact our admin through the Messages page to discuss your request.

04
STEP 04

Confirm the Arrangement

Review your selected services, packages, casket, payment preferences, and other details before confirming your arrangement. If you believe you may be eligible for a discount, you can message our admin through the Messages page to discuss your eligibility and available discounts.

05
STEP 05

Preparation and Coordination

Once the arrangement is confirmed, our team prepares the required services, materials, equipment, and schedule.

06
STEP 06

Service Completion

We carry out the arrangements with care and professionalism, ensuring that the agreed services are properly completed.
